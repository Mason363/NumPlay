## Highlights

* **The game's own look, at the calculator's size:** the big cookie with its shine turning behind it and its squash when you click, the blue background and the cookies falling down it, the cursors circling, the milk rising with your achievements (all 25 flavors), the numbers floating up, the store on the right with the upgrades in their crates and the buildings on their wooden tiles, the news ticker at the top, the game's fonts, icons and pictures.
* **All 20 buildings**, from the Cursor and the Grandma to the Idleverse, the Cortex baker and You, at the game's prices and cookies per second. Buy 1, 10 or 100 at a time, or sell them back. The next ones you can't afford yet show as dark silhouettes and **???**.
* **Every upgrade, 700-odd of them:** the cursors' fingers, every tier of every building's upgrades up to the fifteenth, the synergies, the grandma types (farmer, worker, miner, cosmic... and the elves and bunnies of the seasons) who show up in the grandmas' row, the kittens, the hundreds of flavored cookies, the golden cookie upgrades, the research and the grandmapocalypse's covenant and pledge, the switches, and the fortunes the ticker gives.
* **622 achievements** and the shadow ones, with their names, descriptions and quotes, and the milk they fill.
* **Golden cookies** (Frenzy, Lucky!, Click frenzy, Cookie chain, Cookie storm, the building specials, Dragon Harvest, Dragonflight...) and **wrath cookies** (Clot, Ruin, Elder frenzy, Cursed finger) when the elders are angry; the **grandmapocalypse**, its background turning red, and the **wrinklers** that eat your cookies and give them back, and more, when popped.
* **The buildings' rows**, as in the middle of the game's screen: each building owned on its own background, as many of its pictures as fit, set the game's way, your grandmas named and aged.
* **The Grimoire:** magic that refills by itself, its nine spells (Conjure Baked Goods, Force the Hand of Fate, Stretch Time, Spontaneous Edifice, Haggler's Charm, Summon Crafty Pixies, Gambler's Fever Dream, Resurrect Abomination, Diminish Ineptitude) and their backfires.
* **The Pantheon:** the eleven spirits, the diamond, ruby and jade slots, and the worship swaps that come back with time.
* **Ascension:** the cookie bursts, then the heavens open with **all 129 heavenly upgrades** in their tree to buy with heavenly chips, the permanent upgrade slots, the Born again challenge, and a new run.
* **Sugar lumps** that grow, ripen and level your buildings up; **Krumblor the cookie dragon** and its 21 auras; **Santa**'s 15 stages; the **seasons** of the season switcher, with their reindeer, eggs, hearts and spooky drops.
* **The news ticker**, every English line of it, and **statistics** and **options** as the game has them.
* **Saves:** the game saves itself every minute and when you leave, also in `cookieclicker_saves.py`, so installing the app again doesn't erase your bakery.

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| OK | Click the big cookie |
| Arrows | Choose in the store (up from its top: the news ticker) |
| EXE | Buy (or sell) what is chosen; press the news ticker |
| ÷ | Buy / sell |
| × | 1, 10, 100 at a time (and all, to sell) |
| shift | Click a golden cookie, a wrath cookie or a reindeer |
| var | Pop a wrinkler (three hits) |
| x,n,t | Harvest the sugar lump |
| alpha | The buildings' rows: EXE on one to level it up or open its minigame |
| 1 to 9 | Cast a spell, in the Grimoire |
| toolbox | Statistics |
| ⌫ | Close a note |
| Back | The menu: Options, Statistics, Info, Legacy (ascend), Santa, Krumblor, Keys, Save |
| Home | Save and quit |

On the heavens: the arrows go from upgrade to upgrade, EXE buys one (or chooses a permanent slot's upgrade), shift picks the challenge mode and Back reincarnates.

## Differences

* The calculator has no clock: the game counts the time it runs, so the bakery doesn't bake while it is closed, and **sugar lumps grow 24 times faster** to make up for it. A season from the season switcher lasts 24 hours of play.
* Golden cookies come to the left column, where the big cookie is.
* There is no sound. The Garden and the Stock Market, the farms' and banks' minigames, are not here yet.

This version is written in C for the NumWorks calculator: its pictures, texts, numbers and rules are the game's own (version 2.058), read from the game's public mirror [ozh/cookieclicker](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker) by `tools/fetch.py`, `tools/extract.js` (which runs the game's script for its numbers and texts) and `tools/pack.py` (which packs them into `src/data.bin`). Its fonts are **Merriweather**, **Kavoon** and **Noto Sans** (SIL Open Font License: [Merriweather](../../LICENSES/OFL-1.1-Merriweather.txt), [Kavoon](../../LICENSES/OFL-1.1-Kavoon.txt), [Noto Sans](../../LICENSES/OFL-1.1-NotoSans.txt)). Not affiliated with Orteil or DashNet.

Build: `make` (needs `arm-none-eabi-gcc` and Node.js for nwlink); `make host test` plays it on a computer, and `make data REF=<folder>` packs the data again from what `python3 -I tools/fetch.py <folder>` gathers. Most pictures are packed and unpacked when drawn (`tools/pictures.py`, which can also pack those of `src/data.bin` again, every pixel as it was).
