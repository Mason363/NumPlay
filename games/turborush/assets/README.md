# Original Turbo Rush artwork

The three source atlases in this directory were created for this adaptation with
the built-in **imagegen** tool. They were not extracted from Calcade or another
game. The final game consumes `src/art.h`, generated by `tools/assets.py`.
The large source PNGs are not installed on the calculator.

- `panoramas.png`: four landscape panels, converted to four 400 x 100 panoramas.
- `cars.png`: two rear car body styles, three steering orientations each,
  converted to six 96 x 64 sprites with six recolorable paint palettes.
- `scenery.png`: eight roadside objects, converted to 64 x 112 sprites.

Native images use 6-bit palette indices in flash. Transparent pixels have index
63 for vehicles and roadside objects. Unused quantizer entries are remapped to
valid opaque colors, so black tires and windows do not become holes. Source
alpha is converted to the game's transparency mask. Resizing, slicing and
palette conversion are reproducible; there is no raster editing at runtime.

## Prompt set

### Panoramas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background atlas for a 320x240
retro pseudo-3D arcade racing game, original artwork. A single square atlas with
four full-width panorama strips and no borders or gaps. Each strip contains only
distant scenery and sky, a low horizon and a flat continuous base. No roads,
cars, foreground trees, interface, lettering, watermarks or logos. From top to
bottom: sunny Mediterranean coast with distant lavender mountains and village;
alpine snowy mountains and pine woodland; desert sandstone mesas and canyons;
coastal city skyline with port cranes and towers. Highly polished 1990s arcade
racing pixel art, clustered pixels, warm sunlight, atmospheric perspective,
restrained dithering, readable after reduction to 320x80.

### Cars

Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent car sprite atlas for a
polished retro arcade racer, in a three-column by two-row grid. Top row: the
same red 1980s fictional sports coupe viewed from behind and slightly above,
with slight left yaw, straight rear and slight right yaw. Bottom row: a distinct
low red supercar with wide black spoiler, in those same orientations. All cars
use red body paint for runtime recoloring, black tires, silver exhausts, dark
rear glass, orange-red tail lights and tiny pale plates without text. Detailed
glossy pixel art and readable silhouettes at 90x58. No road, scenery, people,
text, watermark or logos. Actual transparent background.

### Roadside objects

Use case: stylized-concept. Original polished 1990s arcade racing scenery sprite
atlas with actual transparency and a four-column by two-row grid. Top row:
curved-trunk lush palm, tall layered alpine pine, rugged red sandstone formation,
compact gray-blue office building with lit windows. Bottom row: blue roadside
chevron sign with one white right arrow and no words, branching cactus, white
Mediterranean house with terracotta roof, slender curved streetlamp. Each object
isolated, contained and bottom aligned. Detailed cohesive hand-clustered pixel
art readable at 30–70 pixels wide, no heavy black outlines or flat vector style,
no ground plane, grass, backdrop, lettering, watermark or logo.
