# Changelog

What's new in each version of NumPlay. Every file of every version is on the [downloads page](https://github.com/Mason363/NumPlay/releases).

## 1.19.0: Universal Paperclips

- **New on its own:** Universal Paperclips, the whole game from the first clip to the last bit of matter in the universe.
- **NumBlocks:** in the End, triangles point to the Ender Dragon and to the pillars whose crystals are whole, and the dragon no longer comes through the island. Explosions stop at walls, and panes, bars and fences draw faster.
- **Undertale:** Waterfall's walls and floors are told apart and its signs are in reach, the Legendary Artifact brings its Annoying Dogs, and Monster Kid and Undyne keep up when you walk fast.
- **Hollow Knight:** the enemies the game brings back after a rest, a death or a load at a bench come back.
- **Cookie Clicker:** 0.8 MB smaller, drawn the same.
- **NumPlay Studio:** big Game Boy games in Retro-Go, like the 2 MB ones, with Epsilon 26 or Nwagra.
- **Fixed:** Plants vs. Zombies' Wall-nut Bowling plays to its end, Jetpack Joyride always leaves a way past its zappers, and Cookie Clicker's upgrades for baking while closed say they do nothing on the calculator.

## 1.18.0: PolyTrack

- **New on its own:** PolyTrack, all 17 tracks with their loops, wall rides and tubes, on an engine from Boris-TH's PolyGT 3D.
- **Balatro:** rearrange your cards and Jokers with Alpha.
- **Jetpack Joyride:** the bullets, zappers, scientists, Barry's fall and the rooms work as in the game.
- **Celeste:** the Celestial Resort's busiest rooms run at full speed.
- **Fixed:** Hollow Knight's soul orb fills, its first tablet's lesson fits, the Snail Shaman talks after the Elder Baldur, a first charm no longer freezes the Knight and lifeblood cocoons come back after a rest, Celeste no longer shows a hidden passage, and Monkey Mart's cashier stands behind a tall pile of money.

## 1.17.0: Angry Birds, Friday Night Funkin', UNO and Turbo Rush

- **New on their own:** Angry Birds, Friday Night Funkin', UNO and Turbo Rush, by Bobydeluxe.
- **2 Players:** Quick Draw and Tug of War, player 1 on OK, and a fairer computer.
- **NumPlay Studio:** NumVisuals can go in your own NumPlay.
- **Fixed:** Hill Climb Racing's Volcano runs as smooth as the other stages, and Cookie Clicker's save script says what it holds.

## 1.16.0: Monkey Mart, Tomb of the Mask and more Hill Climb Racing

- **New on their own:** Monkey Mart and Tomb of the Mask, by Bobydeluxe.
- **Hill Climb Racing:** the Tractor, Tourist Bus, Super Diesel 4x4, Snow Mobile and Truck, and the Mountain, Mudpool and Volcano.
- **Celeste:** pause and the moves can go on any key.
- **NumPlay Studio:** your calculator's real space (2.44 MB on Epsilon 26, 6.5 MB with Nwagra), big games in French and Chinese NumPlays, and a light calculator for slow computers and phones.
- **Fixed:** the way to Hollow Knight's False Knight is open, and big games keep their saves in a 6 MB NumPlay.

## 1.15.0: Stickman Hook, Donkey Kong, Core Ball, Dune and NumPlay Studio

- **New in NumPlay:** Core Ball, after Tower Blocks.
- **New on their own:** Stickman Hook, Donkey Kong and Dune.
- **NumPlay Studio:** a website where you pick the games in your NumPlay, add your own Game Boy, NES and Sega games, and install it.
- **Fixed:** Hollow Knight's Charms page says to rest at a bench, Home keeps a charm you just put on, and the stag stations are no longer black.
- The four new games were brought to NumPlay by Bobydeluxe. The Upsilon version has 24 games.

## 1.14.0: Suika Game, Flappy Dunk, Tower Blocks, Doodle Jump and Jetpack Joyride

- **New in NumPlay:** Suika Game, Flappy Dunk, Tower Blocks and Doodle Jump, all four by Bobydeluxe.
- **New on its own:** Jetpack Joyride.
- **Fixed:** Subway Surfers' side tracks no longer vanish, Champion Island's last two side quests, Doom's far floors no longer shimmer, and the air hockey puck can't get stuck.
- The Upsilon version has 23 games.

## 1.13.0: nine big games, five new ones in NumPlay

- **New on their own:** Subway Surfers, Five Nights at Freddy's, Doom, The Binding of Isaac, Hill Climb Racing, Plants vs. Zombies, Sonic the Hedgehog, Cookie Clicker and Level Devil.
- **New in NumPlay:** 2 Players, Space Invaders, Sudoku, Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe and Sokoban.
- NumVisuals moves out to an app of its own. Its backgrounds stay in NumPlay.
- **Fixed:** your bug lists for NumBlocks, Doom, Hollow Knight, Undertale, Celeste, NumDrive and Champion Island.

## 1.12.0: Chrome Dino

- **New in NumPlay:** Chrome Dino, after Tetris, brought to NumPlay by LilyTheCatgirl.

## 1.11.2: Celeste's Summit checkpoints, NumDrive faster and fixed levels

- **Fixed:** every checkpoint in Celeste's 7B works again. NumDrive is faster, its shadows stay put, and nine of its levels play as they should.

## 1.11.1: 46 KB smaller

- NumPlay takes 46 KB less space on the calculator. Every game plays and looks as before.

## 1.11.0: Undertale

- **New on its own:** Undertale, the whole Underground to the neutral ending.
- **Fixed:** Tetris' Up is harder to press by accident, and On/Off saves and quits NumBlocks.

## 1.10.0: Block Blast

- **New in NumPlay:** Block Blast, after Buckshot Roulette.
