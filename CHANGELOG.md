# Changelog

What's new in each version of NumPlay. Every file of every version is on the [downloads page](https://github.com/Mason363/NumPlay/releases).

## 1.24.28: Hill Climb Racing on the Casio

- **Casio fx-CG50:** Hill Climb Racing starts.
- **TI-84 Plus CE:** 2 Players' Air Hockey, Tanks and Light Cycles run two to four times faster.
- **NumPlay Studio:** Your files. Your pictures, PDFs, videos and books as apps.

## 1.24.27: Nwagra on Epsilon 26 again

- **NumPlay Studio:** Nwagra on Epsilon 26 is back, with its 6.5 MB. The installer's NaN was a Retro-Go bug, fixed in 1.24.20, not Nwagra.

## 1.24.26: Hollow Knight faster on the Casio

- **Casio fx-CG50:** Hollow Knight and Fungal Wastes run about four times faster, and keep the game's own speed when frames are slow. Every game sends whole frames to the screen, once each, and does its maths in a third to half the time: Suika and Coreball show about twice as many frames.

## 1.24.25: Hollow Knight on the Casio

- **Casio fx-CG50:** Hollow Knight and Hollow Knight: Fungal Wastes start, instead of stopping on a black screen with a System ERROR.

## 1.24.24: NumVisuals' new look

- **NumVisuals:** all 11 backgrounds drawn again from scratch, in smooth colour with soft light and glows, and the clock and your words in Nunito, sharp at any size. Still 30 frames a second.
- **NumPlay:** ⌫ on the home screen has all 11 of NumVisuals' backgrounds, with Warp, Tunnel, Fire, Bokeh and Code Rain.
- **NumDash:** NumDash.nwa has The Seven Seas, Viking Arena and Airborne Robots from Geometry Dash Meltdown, and Nock Em from SubZero, after Dash.
- **NumBlocks:** NumBlocks.nwa has fence gates, and spruce, birch, jungle, dark oak and acacia fences, doors and stairs. Open doors lie where 1.8.8's do.
- **Among Us:** Quick Chat in meetings. Press toolbox, then OK.
- **2 3 4 Players:** computer players. Left and Right on the players screen set how many.
- **Retro-Go:** Mega Drive and Game & Watch games on the TI-Nspire.
- **TI-Nspire:** Flappy Dunk runs four times faster and Rider two to three times faster.

## 1.24.22: The beta testers' fixes

- **TI-Nspire:** numplay_keys.tns sets your own keys for every game, del and the letters included, and the TI-Nspire page lists what alpha, toolbox, x,n,t, EXE and Back are on its keypad. The pad's middle no longer gives OK when pressed with an arrow or just after one. Whole frames go on screen, so nothing tears. Every game's saves are in one file, numplay.sav.tns, so the file browser opens faster.
- **TI-Nspire:** Celeste's chapter 6 runs two to three times faster, which was likely the freeze reported there. NumDash's last levels, Suika and Balatro's background run several times faster, and NumDrive, Coreball and the other games' rounded panels faster too.
- **Friday Night Funkin':** the game over plays the game's own death, Boyfriend's skeleton (in pixels in Week 6) and Pico's fall with Nene's knife, and OK tries again with the original's fade. Boyfriend and Pico face the singer they rap against, instead of turning their backs.
- **Bad Time Simulator:** the heart's hitbox is the original's, no longer more forgiving.
- **Subway Surfers:** Jake runs, jumps, rolls and dodges with the game's own moves.
- **The Final Earth 2:** unique buildings can be given workers in the workers' window.
- **NumDash:** in low detail, Dash keeps its ground and walls.
- **Sudoku:** Auto-check in the pause menu, to play without wrong digits shown.
- **Meowdoku:** OK twice can be set to mark only, EXE placing the cats.
- **Block Blast:** the block in hand leaves the tray, as one picked up in the game.
